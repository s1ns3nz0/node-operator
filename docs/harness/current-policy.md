# Current harness policy

This repository follows the pinned Codex harness baseline from
`codex-harness-s1ns3nz0` while retaining the node-operator policy adapters.

- Ambiguous requirements use the configured Paperthin routing and require
  explicit acceptance before a material product decision.
- Durable artifact changes pass the admissibility route; skipped conditional
  checks require a recorded reason.
- Luna gathers bounded evidence, Terra implements and records applicable
  checks, and Sol owns integration and final admission.
- Knowledge and requirements stewardship are explicit-user, read-only entry
  points. Approved product specifications live under `docs/product-specs/`.
- Observable behavior requires a compact demonstration and independent review;
  material findings block completion.

The source baseline is pinned in `plans/2026-09-11-harness-sync/task-contract.json`.
